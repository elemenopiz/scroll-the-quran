About the study notes

The Deep Study commentary is AI-assisted: drafted with AI drawing on the classical works of tafsir — Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Sa‘di — and reviewed before it shipped. It is study material, not a fatwa and not a substitute for a qualified scholar. Where scholars differ, the app says so. The Quran text and the translations themselves are not AI-generated.
