## What the original does (owner's screenshot, iPhone 402×874 pt; no PNG on disk, so geometry below is measured from it)
In the reader, **every verse page shows the rounded logo card** above the verse (the screenshot is Isaiah 40:31, mid-chapter, with the card at the top), and **tapping the logo card raises a sheet**:
- Sheet: rises to y ≈ 249 pt (covers the bottom 71.5 % of the screen; use a custom `.presentationDetents([.fraction(0.715)])` plus `.large` for Dynamic Type), corner radius ≈ 40 pt continuous, fill one step lighter than the page (`Color.cardBackground`), grabber visible at the top centre (y ≈ 256 pt).
- **Cancel** pill at top-left: x 24…115 pt (91 wide), y 266…306 pt (40 tall), fill `chipBackground`, label "Cancel" ≈ 19 pt regular `textSecondary`. `accessibilityIdentifier("reader.verseMenu.cancel")`.
- **Reference** centred, y ≈ 346 pt baseline region: "Isaiah 40:31" ≈ 20 pt semibold `textPrimary` (ours: "Al-Baqarah 2:255" via `model.reference(for:)`).
- **Verse text** under it: serif ≈ 14 pt, `textSecondary`, centred, up to 4 lines (tail-elided beyond, this is a preview), then a hairline divider at y ≈ 448 pt inset 24 pt each side. Our verse block shows the muted Arabic line above the English per CLAUDE.md rule 5 (this is a verse surface).
- **Rows**, 54 pt each, starting y ≈ 470 pt; icon centred at x ≈ 39 pt (SF Symbol, 22 pt regular, `textPrimary`); label at x ≈ 68 pt, ≈ 18 pt semibold `textPrimary`; chevron `chevron.right` 15 pt semibold `textTertiary` at x ≈ 371 pt; hairline separators between rows inset from the label's x to 24 pt from the right edge. In order:
  1. `lightbulb` **Explain Easier** → a sheet/page titled with the reference showing `unit.explainEasier ?? unit.meaning` for the unit containing this verse (`StudyStore.unit(containing:)` or equivalent), serif body 19 pt, with a "Read the full study" link to Deep Study.
  2. `character.book.closed` **Original Language** → the Arabic Uthmani text of the verse at reading size (not muted: this is the one place it is primary — `Typography.arabicAccent` at 30 pt, RTL, centred, `textPrimary`), the English under it, then the unit's `keyTerms` as rows (Arabic script → gloss, note).
  3. `book` **Deeper Study** → Deep Study for the containing unit, anchored at `meaning`.
  4. `link` **Related Verses** → the unit's `crossReferences` as a list (reference, clause, the verse's own English one line); tapping one opens it in the reader (`model.open(verse:)`) and dismisses.
  5. `iphone` (or `lock.iphone` if available on iOS 17) **Set as Widget Verse** → `UserStore` widget verse action that already exists (`widgetVerseRef`); confirm with the reader's existing toast style ("Widget verse set").
  6. `camera.viewfinder` **Snapshot Verse** with subtitle "Share as an image anywhere" (≈ 15 pt regular `textSecondary`) → the existing `ShareSheetView`.
  Rows 1–4 are study content: gate them exactly as Deep Study is gated (`DiscoverGate` / entitlement check in `FeatureDiscover/Entitlements.swift`; free readers get the paywall). Rows 5–6 are free. Every row `accessibilityIdentifier("reader.verseMenu.<explain|original|deeper|related|widget|snapshot>")`.
- If no study unit contains the verse (should not happen: the corpus is complete; assert in a test over all 6,236 verses), rows 1–4 are hidden.
