2. **Read it through** — `juz-a-day` (existing, 30 days), new `khatm-60` (half a juz a day, 60 days), new `ramadan-khatm` (30 days keyed to the nights: the Ramadan tradition of a full reading, with Laylat al-Qadr's Surah Al-Qadr on day 27 in the `about`), new `hizb-a-day` optional (60 hizb).
3. **The Sunnah of reading** — the practices with the best-attested reports, each `about` naming the source collection in one clause: `al-kahf-fridays` (existing; Friday reading; al-Hakim / al-Bayhaqi), new `mulk-every-night` (Surah Al-Mulk before sleep, 30 nights split in 3–4 ayah readings; Tirmidhi), new `baqarah-nights` (last two ayat of Al-Baqarah, nightly, with Ayat al-Kursi after prayers; Bukhari/Muslim, an-Nasa'i), new `yasin-and-the-heart` (Yasin in 7 days), new `three-quls-morning-evening` (7 days; Abu Dawud/Tirmidhi), new `musabbihat` (the surahs opening with tasbih: 57, 59, 61, 62, 64, 87 — one a day).
4. **Stories of the prophets** — new `prophets-in-the-quran` (21 days: Adam, Nuh, Ibrahim, Yusuf, Musa, Isa, Muhammad ﷺ and the rest, each day the key passages in mushaf order), new `surah-yusuf` (the one complete narrative surah, 7 days).
5. **By theme** — `patience`, `gratitude`, `mercy` (existing), new `tawbah` (repentance, 7 days), new `tawakkul` (trust and reliance, 7 days), new `names-of-god` (14 days: the divine names as the Quran states them, one or two a day with the ayat that carry them — no transliteration in prose; the Arabic name appears only as the muted Arabic line the app already draws), new `duas-of-the-quran` (14 days: the supplications the Quran records: 2:201, 3:8, 7:23, 14:40-41, 20:25-28, 25:74, 28:24, 46:15 … in order).
6. **Memorise** — new `short-surahs-40` (the last 40 surahs, one a day, shortest first), new `long-surahs-foundations` optional.

Rules for every plan:
- `schedule[].refs` must be valid passage refs (surah:ayah or ayah-range; the generator has `refsFor`/`locate` helpers); validate every ref against `Content/quran/surahs.json` ayah counts in a test; readings are whole ayat, never split mid-ayah.
- `lengthDays` = schedule length; `dailyMinutes` computed from the English word count of the day's refs (use the Itani text word counts ÷ 150 wpm, min 3, rounded to 5) — no hand-typed minutes.
- `about` 60–120 words, warm, descriptive, sources named as "reported in …" not as commands; `bestFor` one line; `subtitle` ≤ 6 words; `image` one of the existing cover slugs (`mushaf-page`, `prayer-beads`, `geometric-tile`, `dawn-light`, `lantern`, `ink-wash`, `desert-dune`, `olive-branch`) chosen by mood; reuse is fine; append a table of the covers you WISH existed (slug, subject, mood) to `docs/design/asset-requests.md` under "Reading-plan covers" so the owner can generate them.
- `startHere: true` on exactly the four Start-here plans; the beginner section lists them in the intended order.
- Honorific rule from `docs/tasks/content-author.md` applies to the `about` prose; no legal rulings; no sect named; "God" not "Allah" in English prose.
- Data lives in a readable module (`Tools/content-gen/plans/*.mjs` or inline objects at the top of `build-plans.mjs`); `node Tools/content-gen/build-plans.mjs` regenerates `Content/plans.json` deterministically (run twice, diff empty).
